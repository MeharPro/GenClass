Welcome to GenClass

GenClass lets you control the browser by voice. It starts acting while you are still talking: say “search for weather in Toronto and open the first result” and the search runs before you finish the sentence.

1. Allow the microphone

Chrome asks once. Audio is transcribed on your device by the Moonshine speech model unless you choose Chrome's built-in speech in Settings, which sends audio to Google.

2. Open the side panel

  1. Click the GenClass icon in the toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu).
  2. The first run downloads the decision model (about 57–67 MB) and the Moonshine speech model (about 63–154 MB), once.
  3. Press Start listening, or use Alt+Shift+G. Press Esc on the page, Stop all, or Alt+Shift+K to stop everything.

3. Safe by default

  • Dry run is on at first: GenClass highlights what it would do. Turn on live mode in Settings.
  • Anything that sends, submits, buys, deletes or closes waits for you to say “confirm”.
  • It never types into password or card fields, and never acts on browser pages, banking sites or password managers.
